Fun & Miscellaneous

Fun & Miscellaneous

Games, music playback, Spotify integration, server counters, webhook relays, and bot appearance customization.

Games (slash commands)

A full arcade of slash-command games, solo or with a friend: /8ball, /coinflip, /slots, /blackjack (with split & double down), /higherlower, /hangman, /wordle, /trivia, /rps [member], /tictactoe <member>, /connectfour <member>, /poker <member> (multi-stage Texas Hold'em with community cards), /uno (multiplayer queue with House Rules), and /pool (open lobby for multiple players).

Music

Standard voice-channel music playback: !play <query> to queue a track, !skip, !pause/!resume, !stop, !queue [page] (with clear, move, and remove subcommands), !volume <percentage>, !seek <position>, !repeat <mode>, and !nowplaying. Save your preferred setup with !preset [name], and adjust server-wide playback rules (like DJ role or autoplay) under !settings.

Spotify

Deeper Spotify integration for members who link an account: !spotify login to connect, !spotify play <query> or !spotify vc <query> to play a track (or your current Spotify track) straight into voice chat, plus now/nowplaying, like/unlike, queue, artists/tracks [timeframe] for listening stats, and !spotify code <code_or_url> to render a scannable Spotify code.

Server counters

Auto-updating voice or text channels that display a live stat (member count, boosts, etc.): !counter add <option> [channel_type] [flags] to create one, !counter options to see what stats are available, !counter refresh to force an update, and !counter remove <channel> to delete one.

Webhooks

Send and manage messages through saved webhook identifiers instead of the raw webhook UI — handy for relaying content into a channel under a custom name/avatar: !webhook create <channel> <name>, !webhook send <identifier> <payload>, plus edit, deletemsg, avatar, name, list, and delete. The slash version (/send) exposes the same thing as individual embed fields (title, description, color, footer, image, thumbnail) instead of one raw payload.

Bot customization

Give Pleed a server-specific identity: !customize avatar [image_url], !customize banner [image_url], and !customize bio <text> change how the bot appears in your server specifically, without affecting other servers. Check current settings with !customize view and roll any of them back with !customize reset <option>.

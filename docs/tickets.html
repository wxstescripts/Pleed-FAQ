Tickets

Tickets

A full support-ticket system: panels members click to open a ticket, staff claiming, blacklists, and transcripts-friendly channel naming.

Setting up your first panel

  1. Run !setupticket and follow the interactive wizard — it will ask for a channel to post the panel in and the categories/options members can choose from.
  2. Use !panels any time to see every panel configured in the server, and !editpanel <panel_id> to change options after the fact.
  3. Remove a panel entirely with !ticketdeletepanel <panel_id>.

Working a ticket

Once a member opens a ticket, staff typically:

  • !claim — take ownership of the ticket so other staff know it's being handled (!unclaim to release it).
  • !tadd <member> / !tremove <member> — add or remove a participant from the ticket channel.
  • !rename <new_name> — rename the ticket channel.
  • !tinfo — show ticket metadata (opener, claim status, age).
  • !close [reason] — close out the ticket.

Keeping tickets under control

Block repeat troublemakers from opening new tickets with !tblacklist <target> (accepts a user or a role) and lift it later with !tunblacklist <target>.

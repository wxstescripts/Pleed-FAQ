Voice

Voice

VoiceMaster gives every member their own temporary, fully-controlled voice channel that's created on the fly and cleaned up automatically when it's empty.

Setting it up (staff)

  1. !setupvc [category] — creates the trigger channel members join to spawn their own voice channel, optionally inside a specific category.
  2. !voicemaster category <category> — choose where new temp channels get created.
  3. !voicemaster name <template> — set the default name template for new channels.
  4. !voicemaster limit <amount> — set the default member cap for new channels.
  5. !voicemaster trigger <channel> — manually point at an existing channel as the trigger, instead of creating a new one.

Managing your own channel (members)

Once you join the trigger channel and get your own voice channel, it's yours to control with the vc-prefixed commands:

!vcrename <name>

Rename your channel.

!vclimit <amount>

Set the member limit.

!vcbitrate <kbps>

Set the channel bitrate.

!vclock / !vcunlock

Lock or unlock your channel to new joiners.

!vchide / !vcunhide

Hide or unhide your channel from the channel list.

!vcpermit <member>

Let a specific member into a locked channel.

!vcdeny <member>

Deny a specific member.

!vckick <member>

Kick a member out of your channel.

!vcmute <member> / !vcunmute <member>

Server mute/unmute a member in your channel.

!vctransfer <member>

Hand channel ownership to someone else.

!vcclaim

Claim an abandoned temp channel if the owner left.

!vcinfo

View info about the current temp voice channel.

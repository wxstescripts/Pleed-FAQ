Economy & Leveling

Economy & Leveling

A full virtual economy — wallets, a bank, businesses, a stock market, a shop, casino-style games, and a separate XP/leveling system with role rewards.

The basics

CommandDescription
!balance [member]Check a wallet balance.
!workWork a job to earn money (has a cooldown).
!dailyClaim your daily reward.
!deposit <amount> / !withdraw <amount>Move money between wallet and bank — banked money is safe from !rob.
!pay <member> <amount>Send money to another member.
!richestServer economy leaderboard.

Games & risk

Casino-style ways to grow (or lose) your balance: !coinflip, !slots, !blackjack, !gamble, !risk, and !rob <member> to try stealing from someone else's wallet (banked money is protected).

Businesses & the stock market

Buy a business with !buybusiness <business_type>, then grow it with !hire and !businessefficiency, collect its income with !collect (or !collectall for every business you own), and optionally protect it with !insurebusiness — or sabotage a rival's with !sabotage <target> <business_id>. Separately, !buyshares <symbol> <shares> and !sellshares let you trade on the built-in stock market; check prices with !stocks and your holdings with !portfolio.

Poker

A multiplayer Texas Hold'em table: !pokercreate <buy_in> [max_players] starts a table, others join in, and the host can end it early with !pokercancel.

Leveling & XP

A separate activity-based system: members earn XP for chatting and level up, with optional role rewards at chosen levels.

  • !rank [member] and !leaderboard — check XP progress and the server ranking.
  • !levelreward <level> <role> — grant a role automatically at a given level (!levelrewards to list them all, !levelrewardremove to remove one).
  • !levelchannels add/remove/list — restrict which channels actually grant XP.
  • !setxpcooldown <seconds> — how often a member can earn XP from talking (recommended 30–90s).
  • !leveltoggle / !xptoggle — turn level-up announcements or XP gain off entirely.

Giveaways

Run giveaways with role-based bonus entries and message-count requirements: !create <prize> <duration> <winners> [channel] [messages_required] [roles_bonus_entries]. Use !reroll <message_id> <winners> if a winner doesn't claim their prize, or !end / !delete to finish or cancel one early.
